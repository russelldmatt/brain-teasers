Not hard but I thought this was cute enough to include. Lots of ways to solve it, here's probably the best way?

- Realize that the 45° trangle will have equal side lengths, so let's call that $$x$$.
- Then realize the the ratio of the side lengths of the big triangle will be 3 to 2

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{3}{2} &= \frac{3+x}{x} \\
x &= 6
\end{align*}
$$

The length of the black line is the same as the sum of the two sides of the big triangle, so $$6 + 9 = 15$$.
