Mining unobtainium is hard work. The rare mineral appears in only 1%
of rocks in the mine. But your friend Tricky Joe has something up his
sleeve. The unobtainium detector he’s been perfecting for months is
finally ready. The device never fails to detect unobtainium if any is
present. Otherwise, it’s still highly reliable, returning accurate
readings 90% of the time.

On his first day trying it out in the field, the device goes off, and
Joe happily places the rock in his cart. As the two of you head back
to camp where the ore can be examined, Joe makes you an offer: he’ll
sell you the ore for just $200. You know that a piece of unobtanium
that size would easily be worth $1000, but any other minerals would be
effectively worthless.

Should you make the trade?
