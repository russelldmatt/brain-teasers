Can you make all of the numbers from 1 to 20 using exactly four fours and the operators, +, -, \*, /, factorial.

Four example, here are two valid ways to make 0:

$$4 - 4 + 4 - 4$$

$$(4 + 4) \cdot (4 - 4)$$
